open-urban-analysis

Ask a question about a city, get an answer from free OpenStreetMap data. Street networks, walking isochrones and routing computed with OSMnx, NetworkX, GeoPandas and momepy. No account, no API key. This page shows precomputed runs from the skill. OpenStreetMap can lack facilities that exist: a gap on a map is not proof that a facility is missing.

Code: github.com/dzakybd/open-urban-analysis · Write-up: dzaky.ai.id
